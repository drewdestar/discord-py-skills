## Voice: a sample beats a rule list

An example of the voice beats any list of instructions (Anaconda, "Deslopping
AI Prose"). Before writing a bot's replies, get a voice sample: the user's
own words, or a paragraph in the voice the bot should use, in whatever
language the bot answers in. Calibrate against
the sample first; the rules above apply second. A fabricated voice is just
another kind of slop, so never invent a sample: if there is none, write plain
and short, and ask the user for theirs.
